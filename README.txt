1. Replace "your-email@example.com" in index.html with your official email.
2. Replace the BBK photo placeholder with your professional photograph.
3. Update the academic subjects, qualifications, publications and department information.
4. Replace sample announcement text with your actual notices.
5. Add your PDFs/programs in folders when ready and update the links.
6. Keep the repository public if using GitHub Free Pages.
